# North Pole Run

North Pole Run is a two-stage side-scrolling platform game. Help Santa cross the icy route, collect gifts, fight tax-themed enemies, and reach the chimney at the end of each stage.

## Controls

| Action | Controls |
| --- | --- |
| Move left | A or Left Arrow |
| Move right | D or Right Arrow |
| Jump | W or Up Arrow |
| Shoot | Space (hold to keep firing) |
| Resume after the weapon unlock screen | Enter or click |
| Restart after winning or losing | R |

## Camera

The camera smoothly follows Santa through each level and looks ahead in the direction he is facing. It stays within the level bounds. The distant skyline, nearer skyline, and fog scroll at different speeds to create a parallax effect.

## Levels

**Stage 1: The Frozen Trail** starts the game automatically. Cross gaps, use the raised one-way platforms, collect gifts, and fight the enemy waves. Reach the chimney to continue to Stage 2.

**Stage 2: The Icebound Pass** has a longer route, more gaps and platforms, and tougher, more frequent enemies. Its unique mechanic is a special gift at the start of the stage. Collect it to pause the game and unlock the Triple Blaster. After resuming, holding Space fires a faster spread of three bolts; each bolt deals the blaster's normal damage.

## Selecting a level

There is no level-select menu or secret level-switch key. A new game starts at Stage 1, and reaching its chimney starts Stage 2. Reaching Stage 2's chimney completes the game. Press R on the win or game-over screen to start again from Stage 1.

## Bonus elements

- **Triple Blaster unlock:** A special gift that looks like the regular gifts pauses the game and presents an unlock screen. Collecting it enables faster three-direction firing for the rest of Stage 2.
- **Collectible gifts and health:** Gifts increase the collection counter and restore 5 HP each. The HUD also shows lives, health, and defeated enemies.
- **Enemy variety:** IRS Agents, Auditors, and Tax Collectors have different health, damage, and movement speeds. Stage 2 increases enemy stats and the number of enemies.
- **Lives and recovery:** Santa has three lives, temporary invulnerability after taking damage, and a safe respawn point after reaching solid ground.
- **Atmosphere and effects:** The game draws its skyline, moon, fog, ground, platforms, and chimney in code. Snow-like ash, movement dust, jump and landing effects, collectible sparkles, and hit effects add motion and feedback.
- **Animation and sound:** Santa and enemies use animated sprite sheets. Looping background music and synthesized sound effects play during gameplay.
